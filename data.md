<section class="data-hero" aria-label="Research agenda">
  <p class="data-eyebrow">Research agenda</p>
  <p class="data-lead">
    My research asks who governs, how political elites rise, and how institutions shape their behavior across political systems. I build the data infrastructure needed to answer these questions, combining comparative longitudinal biographies, auditable AI-assisted workflows for scalable data construction, and multimodal measures derived from images, voice, and video.
  </p>
  <p class="data-hero-summary">
    Together, these projects expand the <strong>breadth</strong>, <strong>scale</strong>, and <strong>depth</strong> of comparative research on political elites.
  </p>

    <p class="data-section-kicker">Breadth</p>
    <h2 id="breadth-title">Comparative Political Elites Data</h2>
    <p>
      I am building a modular comparative data infrastructure that traces the careers, education, political affiliations, family ties, and institutional positions of political elites across national and transnational settings. Current modules cover the United States, national governments across the OECD, Russia, ten Southeast Asian countries, international organizations, and Chinese political elites. Modules are released individually while being harmonized to a common schema.
    </p>
  </header>

<section id="scale" class="data-section" aria-labelledby="scale-title">
  <header class="data-section-header">
    <p class="data-section-kicker">Scale</p>
    <h2 id="scale-title">Scalable and Auditable Data Construction</h2>
    <p>
      My methodological work addresses two linked problems in document-based data construction: how relevant evidence is discovered and synthesized, and how complex coding rules are executed consistently across cases. Together, these projects develop source-linked and auditable workflows for turning fragmented documents into structured comparative data.
    </p>
  </header>

  <div class="data-flow" role="img" aria-label="From fragmented and multilingual sources through evidence discovery, codebook execution, expert adjudication, and rule updates to versioned research-ready datasets">
    <div class="data-flow-step"><span>01</span><strong>Fragmented and multilingual sources</strong></div>
    <div class="data-flow-step"><span>02</span><strong>Evidence discovery and source-linked synthesis</strong></div>
    <div class="data-flow-step"><span>03</span><strong>Codebook execution and structured coding</strong></div>
    <div class="data-flow-step"><span>04</span><strong>Expert adjudication and rule updates</strong></div>
    <div class="data-flow-step"><span>05</span><strong>Versioned, research-ready datasets</strong></div>
  </div>

  <!-- Optional future image: /assets/img/data/workflow-evidence-example.png -->

  <div class="data-card-grid data-method-grid">
    <article class="data-card data-method-card">
      <p class="data-method-label">Evidence discovery and synthesis</p>
      <h3>Agentic Framework for Political Biography Extraction</h3>
      <p class="data-question">How can researchers recover reliable biographical evidence from dispersed, multilingual, and unevenly documented sources?</p>
      <p>The project evaluates a two-stage synthesis-and-coding workflow across Chinese, American, and OECD political elites, benchmarking automated data production against human-curated and human-coded records.</p>
      <div class="data-card-footer">
        <span class="data-status data-status-review">Revise &amp; resubmit · AJPS Research Note</span>
        <a class="data-text-link" href="/assets/pdfs/political_fact_extraction_20260305.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Read paper →</a>
      </div>
    </article>

    <article class="data-card data-method-card">
      <p class="data-method-label">Consistent codebook execution</p>
      <h3>The Codebook Is Not a Prompt</h3>
      <p class="data-project-subtitle">Compiling Social Science Coding Rules into Adaptive LLM Workflows</p>
      <p class="data-question">How can complex social-science coding rules be translated into workflows that preserve expert decisions, resolve ambiguity, and support consistent recoding across cases?</p>
      <p>The project treats a codebook as an executable and governed data-production process rather than a single prompt, with structured task decomposition, expert escalation, persistent rulings, and version-aware recoding.</p>
      <div class="data-card-footer">
        <span class="data-status data-status-development">In development</span>
      </div>
    </article>

  <header class="data-section-header">
    <p class="data-section-kicker">Depth</p>
    <h2 id="depth-title">Measuring Elite Behavior Beyond Text</h2>
    <p>
      Biographical records reveal where political elites come from and how their careers unfold, but they do not show how authority, conflict, and emotion are performed in real time. I therefore use official portraits, speech audio, and political video to measure perceived appearance, vocal delivery, and facial affect.
    </p>
  </header>
